Everything You Need to Know About EsDeeKid, the Breakout Rapper of 2025

Liverpool’s elusive newcomer has reached music fans’ feeds with a mask, a distinctive sound and rumors linking him to actor Timothée Chalamet. Here’s what is known about the rapper.

Artist profile · Underground Rap Community

Key takeaways

  • Masked Liverpool rapper EsDeeKid broke through in 2025 with his debut album Rebel and tracks including “Phantom” and “4 RAWS.”
  • Online speculation connected him to Timothée Chalamet; their “4 RAWS” remix brought the two together on record.
  • His sound pairs forceful production with a pronounced Scouse accent. Collaborators include fakemink and Rico Ace.
  • A world tour was planned for early 2026 at the time of the source report.

UK rap’s underground drew new attention in 2025, with artists such as fakemink, Jim Legxacy and skaiwater helping define a wider moment. EsDeeKid became one of its most intriguing new figures.

A masked presence

EsDeeKid keeps his identity private, appearing in a balaclava and giving few interviews. That mystery, alongside the success of “Phantom,” helped fuel online discussion, including speculation about a resemblance to Timothée Chalamet. A Complex report on the rumors tracked how the theory spread.

The conversation took a new turn when the two appeared together on a “4 RAWS” remix. Their collaboration shifted the story from resemblance and guesswork to an actual musical link.

The sound of Liverpool

EsDeeKid’s music stands out for its hard-hitting beats, blown-out low end and off-kilter grooves, set against his unmistakable Liverpool accent. The approach shares some territory with rage rap and the influence of Playboi Carti, while keeping a distinct local character.

His debut album Rebel expanded his audience, with “Phantom” and “4 RAWS” among the tracks drawing attention. Collaborations with artists including fakemink and Rico Ace have also connected him to the wider UK underground. At the time of the report, a world tour was planned for early 2026.

Where is he from?

EsDeeKid is from Liverpool, England. His lyrics reference the city, and he has also posted about building his career from a council estate, a UK term for publicly supported housing. In a June 2025 post, he reflected on his progress and his Liverpool roots.

How much music does he have?

One of his earliest SoundCloud uploads is a May 2024 remix of Rae Sremmurd’s “Black Beatles.” The remix keeps the original’s airy keys but brings in heavier drums and a more forceful vocal approach.

He followed it with loose tracks that pushed toward a darker, rougher sound. “Palaces,” made with Rico Ace, became an early calling card and has since collected more than two million plays on SoundCloud. On June 20, 2025, he released his debut album, Rebel.

What song blew him up?

“LV Sandals,” with fakemink and Rico Ace, helped carry EsDeeKid’s voice beyond his initial audience. Its repeated hook and the contrast between the performers made it one of Rebel’s most immediate tracks.

“Phantom” became a bigger viral moment later in 2025. Its stop-start drum pattern, produced by Wraith9, found a second life in DIY trailer edits and memes on TikTok, alongside other fan-made clips. At the time of the original report, the song had passed 70 million Spotify streams, while Rebel was among the platform’s most-streamed rap albums that week, according to Kworb’s chart data. Streaming figures change over time.

What does he sound like?

The balaclava and medieval-style armor in “Century” match the confrontational edge of EsDeeKid’s music.

His tracks often combine ominous synths, distorted bass and the producer’s recurring “ok” tag. EsDeeKid brings fast, tightly packed verses, bending and slurring the ends of words to keep the energy high. “Panic” and “Mist” are two useful starting points.

Who are EsDeeKid’s closest collaborators?

Producer Wraith9 is central to the sound of Rebel, with the supplied report crediting him across the project. Rapper Rico Ace appears on several of its tracks, and their contrasting deliveries give their collaborations a clear back-and-forth feel.

EsDeeKid has also worked with fakemink since at least late 2024, when he reworked fakemink’s track “Tropical.” Their partnership has continued as both artists gained wider attention.

Who has shown support?

EsDeeKid was announced as an opener for a Yung Lean UK show in an event announcement. He has also been seen with Yeat; a shared photo prompted fans to wonder whether a collaboration might follow, though no release is confirmed.

Other moments that drew attention include UK show connections with Nettspend and OsamaSon, plus a clip of Jack Harlow listening to “LV Sandals.” These co-signs and appearances have helped bring the Liverpool rapper to new audiences.

So, who is he?

EsDeeKid has kept personal details private, and his legal identity has not been publicly confirmed. Rolling Stone covered the identity speculation, but online theories and alleged account links should not be treated as confirmation. His exact age is also unverified; in an X post, he described accomplishing things as a teenager.

Fans have also connected him to the earlier name Dualspines. EsDeeKid appeared on a Dualspines track called “Altered”, and listeners have debated whether the voices match. That remains fan speculation rather than a confirmed alias.

What’s next for the Liverpool rapper?

On November 11, EsDeeKid announced an eight-city world tour planned for early 2026, with dates listed for cities including New York, Chicago, Sydney and Montreal. That announcement reflects the scale of his rise; because the scheduled dates have passed, check the original tour update or official channels for current dates and ticket availability.
